Zsh command-line editing bindings
=================================

Active keymap: Emacs (main aliases emacs).
Captured from a freshly loaded interactive Zsh with your configuration.

Notation:
  ^A       Ctrl+A
  ^[       Escape; usually also the Alt prefix
  ^?       Backspace
  ^I       Tab
  ^M       Enter
  ^X^E     Ctrl+X followed by Ctrl+E
  ^[b      Alt+b, or Escape followed by b

Uppercase and lowercase keys are distinct. Arrow keys and other special keys
send escape sequences that vary between terminals. Ordinary printable
characters use self-insert. Unbound keys use the keymap's default handling.

Inside tmux, Ctrl+Q is your tmux prefix. Press Ctrl+Q twice to pass it to Zsh.

To inspect bindings in your current shell:
  bindkey -M main
