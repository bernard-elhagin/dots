Zsh widgets
===========

239 ordinary widget names from your configured interactive Zsh.
Dot-prefixed names invoke the original versions of the 193 built-in widgets.

The right-aligned column shows bindings in the active main (Emacs) keymap.
^A means Ctrl+A; ^[ means Escape or the Alt prefix; ^? means Backspace.
^I is Tab; ^M is Enter. Sequences such as ^X^E mean Ctrl+X then Ctrl+E.
Escape sequences for arrows and other special keys depend on your terminal.
A blank binding column means the widget has no binding in this keymap.
Inside tmux, press Ctrl+Q twice to send ^Q to Zsh.
